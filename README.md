# Personal Development & Purpose Coaching Platform

> Discover Yourself. Develop Your Potential. Fulfill Your Purpose.

A digital personal-development and coaching platform for girls and women aged **16–40** who want to understand themselves better, discover their strengths and potential, gain clarity about their purpose, develop valuable personal skills, and make meaningful progress toward their goals.

The platform combines **personalized learning, coaching, mentorship, interactive guidance, consultations, progress tracking, and curated resources** into one guided experience.

**User journey:** Self-discovery → Clarity → Personal development → Action → Measurable growth

**Core flow:** Discover → Assess → Set Goals → Get Personalized Path → Learn → Practice → Connect With Coach → Track Growth → Achieve Milestones → Continue Growing

## Target Users

### Primary Users
Girls and women aged 16–40 who:
- Want to discover their strengths and potential
- Are seeking clarity about their purpose
- Want to develop personal and professional skills
- Need guidance, accountability, or mentorship
- Want access to structured personal-development resources
- Prefer having a coach available to guide them

### Coach / Admin
Manages content, users, coaching activities, consultations, resources, and communication.

## Product Goals

Help users to:
1. Understand themselves better
2. Identify strengths, interests, and areas for development
3. Gain clarity about purpose and goals
4. Develop practical personal and professional skills
5. Access relevant learning resources
6. Interact directly with the coach
7. Book and receive personalized coaching
8. Track their personal-development journey
9. Purchase coaching services and resources
10. Build a consistent habit of personal growth

## Key Features

### 1. User Registration & Profile
Account creation with name, age range, interests, goals, areas to improve, career/business interests, and coaching interests. Editable as users grow.

### 2. Personalized Discovery Assessment
Guided onboarding assessment covering strengths, interests, skills, challenges, aspirations, confidence, goals, purpose understanding, and coaching needs. Results in a **personalized starting point**.

### 3. Personalized Growth Dashboard
Central home answering: *“What should I focus on next in my personal-growth journey?”*
- Goals, recommended paths, current activities, progress
- Upcoming sessions, coach messages, recommended resources
- Achievements, Q&A, suggested next steps

### 4. Personalized Learning Paths
Structured development areas:
- **Self-Discovery** – personality, strengths, values, identity
- **Purpose & Vision** – purpose discovery, vision, direction, goal setting
- **Confidence & Self-Development** – confidence, mindset, discipline, emotional growth
- **Communication & Relationships** – communication, boundaries, relationships
- **Career & Business** – career, entrepreneurship, branding, workplace growth
- **Leadership** – influence, decision-making, responsibility
- **Productivity & Goal Achievement** – planning, habits, time management

### 5. Learning Content
Video, audio, written lessons, exercises, worksheets, reflection questions, guides, recommended books, and downloadable resources. Users can save favorites.

### 6. Coaching & Mentorship
- **Ask the Coach:** submit questions, get guidance and resource referrals
- **One-on-One Coaching:** select service → pick time → describe needs → pay → confirm → attend → follow-up
- **Group Coaching:** scheduled topic-based sessions
- **Structured Programs:** e.g. Discover Your Purpose, Building Confidence, Career Clarity, Personal Transformation

### 7. Personal Growth Journey
Unified view of goals, paths, lessons, exercises, reflections, sessions, feedback, milestones, and achievements — focused on holistic growth, not just “% complete”.

### 8. Goals & Action Plans
Define goals, set timeframes, break into actions, track completion, record reflections, review progress, with content/coaching recommendations.

### 9. Reflection & Journaling
Private personal reflection journal for learnings, self-discoveries, challenges, insights, progress, and questions for the coach.

### 10. Coach Feedback
Personalized feedback after assessments, exercises, consultations, or programs — observations, improvements, resources, next steps, encouragement.

### 11. Resource & Book Store
Browse and purchase books, courses, downloadable resources, and coaching programs. View purchase history.

### 12. Payments
For consultations, group coaching, programs, courses, books, and resources — with confirmation and access provisioning.

### 13. Notifications & Reminders
Upcoming sessions, uncompleted activities, new coach responses, recommendations, goals, and milestones — useful, not overwhelming.

### 14. Community & Engagement (post-MVP)
Moderated discussions on purpose, confidence, growth, career, relationships, leadership. Safeguarding controls for ages 16–17.

### 15. Coach / Admin Dashboard
Manage users, content (courses/lessons/exercises/articles/books), coaching (services/availability/bookings/programs/Q&A), payments, progress/feedback, and communications/announcements.

## How It Works

1. **Discover Yourself** – Complete your personal discovery assessment
2. **Define Your Goals** – Identify what you want to achieve
3. **Follow Your Growth Path** – Get personalized recommendations
4. **Get Coaching** – Ask questions, book consultations, join programs
5. **Track Your Growth** – Reflect, complete activities, celebrate milestones

## Product Principle

> Not a library of courses — a **personal growth companion guided by a coach**.

Every feature connects back to:
**Who am I? → What am I capable of? → What is my purpose? → What do I need to develop? → What should I do next? → How am I progressing?**

## MVP Scope (v0.1.0)

1. User registration / profile
2. Personal discovery assessment
3. Goal setting
4. Personalized dashboard
5. Personalized learning paths
6. Educational content
7. Ask the Coach
8. One-on-one consultation booking
9. Payments
10. Personal growth / progress tracking
11. Coach / Admin dashboard
12. Resource / book marketplace

Post-MVP: group coaching, structured programs, community, advanced journaling.

## Vision

> A trusted digital space where every girl and woman can discover who she is, understand her potential, develop herself, gain clarity about her purpose, and receive the guidance and resources she needs to take meaningful action.
